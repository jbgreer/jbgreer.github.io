About this website

Perhaps like some of you, I have tried various methods to preserve information I've run across, often on the web during research or reading. I have a couple of systems, including Obsidian, which I use for local storage of longer form documentation and research.

I have an Apple iPhone and iPad. A few years ago I started storing links to items of interest using the Apple Reminder App. While that's a good app for storing a working list of reminders and is shared across both devices, it's not well suited to long-term storage.

At the same time, I've had several failed attempts at blogging over a couple of decades. I think part of that is down to deciding on a format. I have always enjoyed Leah Neukirchen's Trivium, and Anarchaia before that. If you're not aware, Anarchaia was the inspiration for Tumblr. I've never asked, but I like to think that it captures not only interested links, and obviously links that Leah thinks are worthy of being shared, but also captures a sense of joy.

So, after only 20 years of following, I've decided that a tumblelog of a sort would suit my purposes. I've exported some old Hugo blog entries and written a Clojure app to reformat them for Cryogen. I grabbed an Apple Shortcuts that exports entries from my Reminders, and wrote another Clojure app to format those entries. Going forward my goal is to export on a weekly or monthly basis and republish here. While many of my links are mundane, I also hope to capture a little joy in some of the things I've found, and hope that you find some of them worth sharing.

Why Clojure? I like it.
